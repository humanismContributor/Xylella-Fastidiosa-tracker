# Xylella-Fastidiosa-Olive-Groves-tracker
An interactive dashboard to track the spread of Xylella Fastidiosa in the Mediterranean basin

## Suggest an official source

The tracker checks official sources every week for news about Xylella fastidiosa: EPPO, the EU list of demarcated areas, EFSA's host plant database, and national and regional plant health services. New outbreaks they report are added to the map automatically and shown with a dashed outline until a maintainer has checked them against the source. If you know an official page that should be watched, such as a ministry, a regional plant health service or an EU agency, please suggest it:

**[Suggest an official source](https://github.com/humanismContributor/Xylella-Fastidiosa-tracker/issues/new?template=official-source.yml)** (needs a free GitHub account)

The form asks for the page address and what to watch on it. A maintainer reviews each suggestion; once approved, the page is tested automatically and added to the weekly check.
